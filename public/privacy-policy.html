Privacy Policy for LendFlow

Last Updated: May 8, 2026

Introduction

LendFlow, operated by Rakeer Solution ("we", "our", or "us"), is a loan management system. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our services.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Address
  • Loan details (amount, tenure, interest rate)
  • Payment history
  • Identity documents (for verification)

Automatically Collected Information

  • Login timestamps
  • IP addresses
  • Device information
  • Usage data

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and manage loans
  • Send payment reminders via WhatsApp
  • Send payment confirmations via WhatsApp
  • Provide customer support
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Improve our services

WhatsApp Communications

We use WhatsApp Business API to send you:

  • Loan approval notifications
  • Payment confirmations
  • Due date reminders
  • Account updates

You can opt out of WhatsApp notifications by contacting us.

Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service providers (payment processors, cloud hosting)
  • Legal authorities (when required by law)
  • Your employer/company (if applicable)

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Secure cloud storage (Firebase)
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security audits

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request data deletion
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Export your data

Data Retention

We retain your data:

  • Active loans: Duration of loan + 7 years (legal requirement)
  • Completed loans: 7 years from completion
  • Marketing data: Until you opt out

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Authentication
  • Session management
  • Analytics
  • Performance monitoring

Third-Party Services

Each service has its own privacy policy.
